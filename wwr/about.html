Splash White Water Rafting

Our purpose is to provide thrilling and unforgettable rafting experiences while ensuring safety and fun for everyone.

History

Founded in 1995 by a group of adventure enthusiasts, Splash White Water Rafting has grown into a leading provider of unforgettable outdoor experiences. We started with just a handful of rafts and a passion for exploration.

Over the decades, we have expanded our operations, trained hundreds of expert guides, and introduced thousands of guests to the thrill of white water. Our commitment to safety and fun has never wavered.
